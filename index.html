01 / Independent project

Study Together

A live study-room website I started building so friends can study together online. I wanted it to feel simple and welcoming: join a room, see who's around, and keep a shared sense of progress while everyone works.

What I built

  • Room-based study spaces for a small group.
  • Custom names and avatars, plus online presence.
  • Room chat and a shared lo-fi music concept.
  • Study-time tracking and a leaderboard concept.
  • A dark, welcoming intro screen and custom visual direction.

The process

I connected the front end to Supabase and worked with its database and real-time features. While testing with another account, old test records remained in a table; I investigated the database and cleared the relevant records. That helped me understand why test data and shared state matter when building a live product.
